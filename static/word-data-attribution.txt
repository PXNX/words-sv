WordCircle word-data attribution

Selected German noun candidates are derived from gambolputty/german-nouns,
compiled from the German Wiktionary and made available under CC BY-SA 4.0:
https://github.com/gambolputty/german-nouns

The repeatable frequency ranking uses the documented OpenSubtitles CISTEM list:
https://github.com/olastor/german-word-frequencies

The common/rare classification follows the frequency-class framework described by Duden:
https://www.duden.de/hilfe/haeufigkeit
