# Playable-language starter packs

The new French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, and Ukrainian packs are deliberately small, curated **starter pools**. Their content is grouped by the app’s existing A1–C2 progression controls, but the labels are practical learning progression markers rather than a claim of official word-by-word certification. This avoids representing a thin set of manually curated entries as a comprehensive official curriculum.

French selection and level review follow the availability of FLELex, a French-as-a-foreign-language lexical resource with per-level CEFR frequencies derived from textbooks and graded readers. Broader language-pack review is informed by the documented uneven availability of multilingual CEFR data; the project therefore retains a transparent, conservative starter-pack scope rather than copying a restricted corpus.

Each pack includes native-language definitions and optional grammatical articles. Articles are shown only as a display layer; the stored answer and spaced-repetition key remain the underlying uppercase lemma.
